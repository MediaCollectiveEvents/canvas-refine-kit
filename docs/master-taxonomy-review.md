# Media Collective Master Taxonomy — review draft

This is a proposed reconciliation, not the active website taxonomy. Review the additions before connecting to CMS or Event Hub. Existing `taxonomy.json`, `keywords.json`, `topics.json` and original documentation remain unchanged.

## Counts

- 7 existing functional areas
- 13 existing themes + 3 proposed themes
- 30 recovered keywords + 36 proposed keywords

## Rules

- Use typed identifiers (`area`, `theme`, `keyword`); retain legacy IDs and event mappings for migration. The sole renamed canonical keyword is `ai-automation` -> `ai-powered-automation`; the existing theme keeps its ID.
- Keywords may belong to multiple themes. Functional areas describe work, not interests, and are selected separately.
- Keep source evidence separate from definitions and aliases; aliases are matching suggestions, not additional canonical tags.
- Do not infer speaker expertise from attendance, article authorship or proposed session participation.
- No automatic public tagging or database changes until approval. Review terms for overlap (especially monetisation vs content monetisation and trust vs provenance), then add validation, CMS management and contributor suggestions with human approval.
- Source report titles and occurrence counts were not recovered. OFF AIR editorial evidence still requires audio verification before quotation or attribution. No fresh external research was performed in this pass.
